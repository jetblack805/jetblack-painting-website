### What is covered

Jetblack Painting warrants the **workmanship** of its painting work for **five (5) years** from the
date of practical completion. ⚠️ **CONFIRM: does the five years run from practical completion, or
from the final invoice date?**

Within that period, if the paint finish fails in a way caused by our workmanship — ⚠️ **CONFIRM
this list** — we will rectify it at our cost. Typical workmanship failures are:

- flaking, peeling or blistering caused by inadequate surface preparation
- coatings applied over a surface that was not properly cleaned, sanded, filled or primed
- runs, sags, holidays or missed areas in the applied finish
- failure of a coating system we specified for the surface it was applied to

### What is not covered

⚠️ **CONFIRM this entire list — exclusions are the part most likely to be wrong.**

- normal wear, fading, chalking or weathering consistent with the coating's age and exposure
- failure of the underlying substrate: rot, rust, render cracking from structural movement,
  rising damp, or moisture entering through a building fault such as a failed roof, gutter,
  flashing or seal
- damage from storm, hail, impact, fire, vandalism or accident
- surfaces we advised in writing were not suitable for painting, where the customer asked us to
  proceed anyway
- work altered, over-coated or repaired by anyone other than Jetblack Painting
- materials supplied by the customer
- paint product defects, which are covered by the manufacturer's own warranty
  ⚠️ **CONFIRM: Dulux and Taubmans product warranties are separate from this one — do you want
  that stated, and do you want to help customers claim on them?**

### Mandatory text required by Australian Consumer Law

> Our goods and services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian
> Consumer Law. For major failures with the service, you are entitled: to cancel your service
> contract with us; and to a refund for the unused portion, or to compensation for its reduced
> value. You are also entitled to be compensated for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or
> damage. If the failure does not amount to a major failure, you are entitled to have problems
> with the service rectified in a reasonable time and, if this is not done, to cancel your
> contract and obtain a refund for the unused portion of the contract.

**This block is not optional.** A warranty against defects given to a consumer must carry this
wording. It sits alongside the warranty above — it does not replace or reduce it.
