Frequently asked questions about painters in Safety Beach

How do you protect Safety Beach homes from salt air?

Salt is the main enemy on this stretch of the bay. Before painting we wash the salt film off the exterior — paint will not bond properly through it — then prime bare timber and finish with flexible, salt-resistant exterior systems. Metal fixings, gutters and balustrades near the water get particular attention because that's where corrosion starts.

Do you paint homes around Martha Cove and the marina?

Yes. Properties around Martha Cove sit right on the water, which means constant salt spray, strong UV and wind-driven rain. Those homes need coastal-grade preparation and premium salt-resistant topcoats, and we're happy to work with owners corporations on multi-dwelling and common-area work.

How often should a Safety Beach home be repainted?

Exposed coastal homes here generally need repainting more often than inland ones — commonly every 5 to 8 years for exteriors, depending on how directly the property faces the bay. Chalking, flaking around window frames and bare timber showing through are the signs it's due.
