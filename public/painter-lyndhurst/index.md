## First-Cycle Repaints on Lyndhurst's Estate Homes

A builder's original paint job is not a repaint. It is two quick coats applied to a program, over render that in many cases had barely finished curing, and it is specified to pass handover rather than to last twenty years. That is why so many Lyndhurst homes look tired at fifteen while the house itself is still in good order — the substrate is fine, the coating on it has simply done its time. A proper repaint is mostly preparation: washing off the chalk, spot-priming where the original film has failed, and getting a full-build system on rather than another thin one.

The exposure here does not help either. Lyndhurst is flat, open and short on mature tree cover, so north and west elevations take direct sun and wind-driven rain with nothing in the way. Those elevations reliably go first, and on a house where the render, the garage door and the entry door are all different materials, they go at different rates. We quote the whole facade but we tell you honestly which parts are urgent and which will hold another season, rather than pricing everything as though it is all equally gone.

## Frequently asked questions about painters in Lyndhurst

### Our Lyndhurst house is about fifteen years old and has never been repainted. Is that normal?

It is completely normal, and it is most of what we do here. Lyndhurst's housing is young — the bulk of it went up through the 2000s and 2010s — so a large share of the suburb is reaching its first repaint at roughly the same time. The original builder's coating is usually two thin coats sprayed on quickly at handover, which is enough to look right on move-in day and not much more. What tells you it is due is chalking on the north and west render, timber-look entry and garage doors going grey and dry, and fascias and gutters that have lost their gloss.

### Do you paint rendered estate homes in Lyndhurst?

Yes, and a modern estate home is rarely one surface. A typical Lyndhurst facade combines rendered walls, a Colorbond garage door and roof, aluminium window frames, and a timber or timber-look front door — four materials that each need their own preparation and their own coating system. Putting one product across the lot is the single most common reason a repaint on these homes fails early. We scope each element separately and the quote itemises it that way.

### Our estate has design guidelines. Does that change anything for a repaint?

It might, and we check rather than assume. Some estates carry design guidelines that outlast the original build and can limit exterior colour changes on street-facing elevations, while plenty of others only ever applied at construction and no longer apply to you at all. Before we quote a colour change we ask which estate you are in and confirm what, if anything, still binds — so you find out before the job, not during it.
