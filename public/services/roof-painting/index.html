How long does roof painting last?

A properly prepared and coated roof should last 10 to 15 years depending on the substrate, coatings used and maintenance. We use premium roof coating systems for maximum durability.

Do I need to repaint my roof?

Signs your roof needs painting include fading colour, visible moss or lichen, powdery surface texture, or cracked ridge capping. A professional inspection will confirm whether repainting is needed.
