Kitchen Cabinet Resurfacing FAQs

How much cheaper is resurfacing than a new kitchen?

Cabinet resurfacing typically costs a fraction of a full kitchen renovation because the layout, carcasses, and benchtops stay in place.

How long does cabinet resurfacing take?

Most kitchens are completed within about a week, including preparation, priming, and 2-pack topcoats.

Is a 2-pack finish durable in a kitchen?

Yes. 2-pack polyurethane is a hard-wearing, wipeable finish designed for high-use surfaces like kitchen cabinetry.
