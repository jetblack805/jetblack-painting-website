Jetblack Painting FAQs

Do you offer free painting quotes?

Yes. Jetblack Painting provides free, no-obligation painting quotes across Melbourne.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Jetblack Painting is fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial painting work.

What Melbourne areas do you service?

We service Melbourne suburbs including Mordialloc, Brighton, Toorak, Bentleigh, Mentone, Kingston, Bayside, Greater Dandenong, and surrounding areas.

How long does interior painting usually take?

Most interior painting projects take a few days to a week depending on the size, access, and preparation required.

Do you handle exterior and commercial painting too?

Yes. We complete interior, exterior, roof, cabinet, pre-sale, rental, and commercial painting projects.

Can you help with colour selection?

Yes. We can help narrow down colour and sheen choices that suit the property, light, and desired finish.
