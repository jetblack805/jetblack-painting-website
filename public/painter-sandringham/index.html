How do you protect Sandringham homes from salt air and coastal conditions?

Salt air from Port Phillip Bay is a real threat to paintwork along the Sandringham foreshore. We start every coastal job with a thorough pressure wash to remove salt deposits and contaminants before any paint goes on. Skipping or rushing preparation is why coastal paint jobs fail early; we don't cut corners on it.
