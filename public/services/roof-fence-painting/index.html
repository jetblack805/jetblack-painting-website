Why Melbourne chooses Jetblack Painting for roof & fence painting

Roofs and fences take the most weather of any surface on a property. The right preparation and exterior-grade coatings protect them for years and transform how the property presents from the street.

Roof & Fence Painting FAQs

Can you do the roof and fences in one project?

Yes. We regularly quote roof and fence painting together as a combined exterior refresh.

Do you stain fences as well as paint them?

Yes. Depending on the timber and the look you want, we can apply paint systems or exterior stains.

How long does fence painting last?

With proper preparation and exterior-grade products, fence coatings typically last 5–10 years depending on exposure.
